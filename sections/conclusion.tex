\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Seven fields that do not cite one another, reliability, single-cell and
spatial genomics, differential privacy, programmatic weak supervision,
electronic-health-record phenotyping, and multiple instance learning,
each face the same problem:
inference for a latent parameter from data coarsened by a mechanism.
Each, on reaching its central structural result, rediscovers the same
consistency identity and gives it a local name, and each reaches for the
same remedy, a singleton report that pins the latent value, when
identifiability fails.

This paper states the shared results once. The general rank condition
(\cref{thm:general-rank}) and the singleton-restoration result
(\cref{prop:singleton}) say once, with no seam across the seven domains,
when the latent parameter is identifiable and how a singleton restores
it by restoring column rank, with the per-domain singletons, spike-ins,
single-cell probes, gold labels, non-private releases, chart review, and
singleton bags, tabulated as one device. The general consistency theorem
(\cref{thm:general-consistency}) says that at an interior maximum of the
face-value likelihood the fitted mean of the coarsening-sufficient
statistic equals its empirical mean; in a regular exponential family
this is the exact finite-sample mean-value identity and recovers
cell-total, spot-level, code-frequency, bag-prevalence, and (under a
sufficiency-complete parametrization) agreement consistency, while in a
location family it is
exact at a single report and a population first-moment identity that
recovers release consistency.

The synthesis holds, and the boundary of its consistency theorem is
located exactly rather than asserted: differential privacy belongs to
the location-family branch, where the sample-mean form of the identity
holds if and only if the kernel is Gaussian (and the Gaussian case is
itself the exponential-family branch), so release consistency is the
exact single-release identity; and weak supervision reduces exactly only
under a parametrization practitioners do not use, asymptotically
otherwise. The empirical weight stays with the siblings, which are
cited. What the synthesis adds is the recognition that one statistical
idea has been doing the work in all seven places, together with an exact
account of how far each form of it reaches.
