\section{The domains as instances}
\label{sec:applications}

Each domain is one instantiation of \cref{sec:framework,sec:consistency,sec:identifiability}.
We give one short subsection per domain, fixing the latent variable,
the report, the coarsening-sufficient statistic, and the singleton, and
citing the sibling for the domain-specific development, validation, and
data analysis. Nothing here is re-derived.

\subsection{Reliability: masked causes in series systems}

The latent $\Y$ is the failed component $K_i$ of a series system; the
report is a candidate set $c_i$ of suspect components produced by a
diagnostic; the singleton is a diagnostic that resolves the cause. C1
is diagnostic competence, C2 is structural (group-level) masking, C3 is
calibration fixed before failures are observed. This is the
foundational paper \citep{towell2026masked}, which supplies the
C1--C2--C3 likelihood, the augmented-candidate-set rank condition
(\cref{thm:general-rank}), and the regularity conditions the abstract
process inherits. The robustness of estimates to C2 violations, with
sensitivity bands, is \citet{towell2026mdrelax}.

\subsection{scRNA-seq: dropout as coarsening}

The latent $\Y$ is the true transcript count; the report is the
observed count, coarsened by a dropout that maps some positive counts
to zero; the coarsening-sufficient statistic is the count $X$; the
singleton is an ERCC spike-in of known input. Cell-total consistency
(\cref{cor:scrna}) is the regime-(A) identity for the ZINB family. The
sibling \citep{towell2026scrnacoarsening} adds a bias bound for the
spike-in-fit oracle as a function of the ERCC-versus-endogenous capture
ratio, validates cell-total consistency on simulated data and on Tabula
Muris tissue \citep{tabula2018single}, and positions the result
against the zero-inflation controversy \citep{jiang2022zero}.

\subsection{Spatial transcriptomics: deconvolution as coarsening}

The latent $\Y$ is the cell-type composition of a spot together with
cell-type-specific expression; the report is the spot's mixed
expression vector; the coarsening-sufficient statistic is the spot
count $X_{sj}$; the singleton is a single-cell-resolution probe. The
spot-level identity (\cref{cor:spatial}) is the regime-(A) identity for
the spot Poisson model, and the rank condition specializes to full
column rank of the spot-by-cell-type composition matrix $P$. The
sibling \citep{towell2026spatialcoarsening} works out the rank
condition, the consistency identity, and the relation to RCTD
\citep{cable2021robust} on spatial data \citep{stahl2016visualization}.

\subsection{Differential privacy: the noised release as coarsening}

The latent $\Y$ is the true query value $q(D)$ and the functional
$\theta$ behind it; the report is the noised release $M = q(D) + Z$;
the coarsening-sufficient statistic is $M$ itself; the singleton is a
non-private release. This domain sits in regime (B): release
consistency (\cref{cor:dp}) is the location-family first-moment
identity, not the exponential-family one. The coarsening is engineered,
so C3 holds by construction (the curator publishes the scale), C2
partitions the mechanisms (data-independent mechanisms satisfy it,
data-dependent ones violate it), and the sibling
\citep{towell2026dpcoarsening} adds a compositional identifiability
calculus for adaptive composition and a simulation. The
analyst-side framing complements the curator-side statistical-DP
literature \citep{wasserman2010statistical,dwork2014algorithmic}.

\subsection{Weak supervision: votes as coarsening}

The latent $\Y$ is the true label; the report is the vote vector of the
labeling functions; the coarsening-sufficient statistic is the pairwise
agreement indicator; the singleton is a gold-labeled example. Agreement
consistency (\cref{cor:weaksup}) is the regime-(A) identity under a
sufficiency-complete parametrization and asymptotic for the naive-Bayes
label model of data programming \citep{ratner2016data}, whose ancestor
is the Dawid--Skene error-rate model \citep{dawid1979maximum}. The
sibling \citep{towell2026weaksupcoarsening} works out the conditional-
independence identifiability theorem, the rank deficit induced by
labeling-function dependence, and the gold-set sample complexity.

\subsection{Phenotyping: diagnostic codes as coarsening}

The latent $\Y$ is the patient's true disease state; the report is a
billing or diagnostic code; the coarsening-sufficient statistic is the
code indicator $C$; the singleton is a chart-reviewed patient.
Code-frequency consistency (\cref{cor:phenotype}) is the regime-(A)
identity for the single-code Bernoulli model, and the
chart-review-calibrated estimator is the Rogan--Gladen prevalence
correction \citep{rogan1978estimating} with the two error rates
estimated rather than assumed; the two-code, no-chart-review corner is a
Hui--Walter latent-class model \citep{hui1980estimating}. The sibling
\citep{towell2026phenotypecoarsening} develops the glass ceiling, the
identifiability-with-chart-review theorem, and the informative-coding
bias bound.
