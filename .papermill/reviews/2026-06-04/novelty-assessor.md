## Novelty claim and its delineation

The novelty claim is honestly scoped and well defended: "The claim is
unification, not new mathematics" (introduction.tex l.102). The contribution
is (i) recognizing that one consistency identity and one rank/singleton
device recur across six fields as CAR instances, (ii) the explicit reduction
of five named theorems to one statement with an exact account of two regimes,
and (iii) the discovery that DP forces a structurally distinct
location-family regime. This is the correct genre framing for Statistical
Science, whose mandate is unity of the field and which explicitly licenses
"technical results placed in a larger review context."

Delineation from each domain's literature: clean. The applications section
(applications.tex) cites each domain's own anchors (Jiang 2022, Cable 2021,
Dwork-Roth 2014, Ratner 2016, Rogan-Gladen 1978, etc.) and the corresponding
sibling, and states "Nothing here is re-derived." The intro is explicit that
the five named theorems already exist in the siblings and that the
contribution is to show they are one. There is no overclaim of having
invented the domain results.

Delineation from prior coarsening reviews: the discussion's "Relation to the
coarsening-at-random literature" (discussion.tex l.104-117) correctly states
that the CAR conditions are classical (Heitjan-Rubin, Gill-van der
Laan-Robins) and that what is new is the cross-domain recurrence plus the
general rank/singleton apparatus, not the CAR conditions. The
exponential-family mean-value identity that powers regime (A) is correctly
conceded as textbook. This is the right disclaimer set.

## The strongest referee objection is answered

The "isn't regime (A) just textbook exponential-family moment-matching"
objection, the top framing risk in the first review, is now named and
answered in a dedicated paragraph (introduction.tex l.126-146) with three
reasons: (i) the coarsening makes the OBSERVED marginal an exponential family
while the bias lives in the LATENT parameter, so the identity is about what a
fitted latent model structurally cannot detect; (ii) the singleton/rank half
is not elementary moment-matching; (iii) the DP-forces-regime-(B) discovery
is a result about the reach of the principle. This converts the easiest
rejection into a stated strength. Well executed.

## Is the novelty sufficient for the venue?

Yes for Statistical Science and Statistics Surveys (synthesis is the
mandate). The novelty is organization, and the paper is explicit about that,
which is exactly what these venues want. For the proofs-bearing backups
(JASA T&M, JRSS-B, Biometrika) the novelty-as-organization framing is weaker
and would need the general theorem upgraded to a headline methodological
advance; that is a known repositioning cost, not a defect of the current
submission.
